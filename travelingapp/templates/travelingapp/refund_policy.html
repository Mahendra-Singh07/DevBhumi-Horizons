1. Advance payment

To confirm a booking we collect a fixed advance of ₹{{ POLICY.advance|floatformat:"0g" }} through Razorpay. A booking is marked paid only after Razorpay confirms the payment.

2. If you cancel

You can cancel from My Bookings after logging in. What you get back depends on the date you cancel:

  • {{ POLICY.window_days }} or more days before the travel date: {{ POLICY.refund_percent }}% of the advance (₹{{ POLICY.refund_amount|floatformat:"0g" }}) is refunded.
  • Fewer than {{ POLICY.window_days }} days before the travel date: no refund.
  • Booking not yet paid: you can cancel it; nothing was charged, so nothing is refunded.
  • Travel date already passed: the booking can no longer be cancelled.

Days are counted by calendar date, from the day you cancel to the travel date.

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually take 5 to 7 business days to show up, depending on your bank.

4. If we cancel or change a trip

If we have to cancel or reschedule a departure for safety reasons (for example bad weather or road closures), we will contact you using the email or phone number on your booking and offer a new date or a refund of the advance.

5. Payment problems

If money was deducted but your booking still shows as unpaid, write to us at {{ SITE.email }} with your booking ID and, if you have it, the Razorpay payment ID. We will check it and fix it.
