This policy explains what personal information {{ SITE.name }} collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We aim to follow the principles of India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Account: first name, last name, email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Sign in with Google: if you use it, we receive your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile (optional): phone number, address, date of birth and profile picture.
  • Bookings: name, email, phone number, chosen package, travel date, number of travellers and any special requests you write.
  • Payments: payments are handled by Razorpay. We store the order, payment and refund IDs and the payment status. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Newsletter: the email address you enter in the footer form.
  • Verification codes: one-time codes sent by email. They expire after 10 minutes.
  • Contact form: this form opens WhatsApp on your own device with a pre-filled message. Nothing from it is sent to our server.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email.
  • To process bookings, payments, cancellations and refunds.
  • To send service emails: verification codes, password reset codes, invoices and cancellation notices.
  • To reply to your questions and run the trips you book.
  • To send updates only if you subscribed to the newsletter.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the site. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Razorpay for payments and refunds.
  • Brevo to deliver our transactional emails.
  • Cloudinary to store and serve uploaded images, including profile pictures.
  • Sentry to detect and fix errors in the site. Error reports do not include passwords, form contents or payment details.
  • Google for Sign in with Google, Google Fonts and the embedded map.
  • Our hosting and database provider to run the website and store its data.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) for icon fonts, and Pexels for a background video on one page. Your browser contacts these directly when the page loads.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies: a session cookie that keeps you logged in and a security (CSRF) cookie that protects our forms. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Embedded services such as Google and Razorpay may set their own cookies when you use them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. Booking and invoice records are kept for as long as we need them for accounting, dispute handling and legal reasons, even if you ask us to delete your account.

6. Security

The site uses HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to stored data is limited. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your choices

  • View and update your details on your Profile page.
  • To ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion of your account, or to leave the newsletter, email {{ SITE.email }}. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

This site is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page.
