Our Story

From Local Trails to a Trusted Travel Brand

DevBhumi Horizons started with a simple idea — the hidden corners of Uttarakhand deserved more thoughtful, honest travel experiences. What began as a handful of guided treks for friends and family has grown into a full-fledged travel company serving thousands of travelers across India and abroad.

Every itinerary we design is rooted in local knowledge, built by people who grew up on these mountains, walked these trails, and know exactly what makes a journey feel safe, soulful and unforgettable.

Our Mission

To craft safe, affordable, and memorable journeys across Uttarakhand while promoting sustainable, community-first tourism.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted gateway for travelers seeking the authentic soul of the Himalayas — one honest trip at a time.

Our Values

Transparency, local partnership, safety-first planning, and genuine care for every traveler who joins us.
